Liuli Society of America brings together natural wellness education, traditional knowledge, research, and community engagement.

Our mission

A thoughtful bridge between knowledge and everyday well-being.

LSOA promotes education, research, and community engagement related to natural wellness and traditional knowledge. We encourage responsible learning and dialogue between traditional perspectives, contemporary research, and lived experience.

Our approach is educational rather than diagnostic or therapeutic. We aim to preserve knowledge, encourage inquiry, and create accessible opportunities for people to learn.

Education

Sharing knowledge that encourages thoughtful, informed approaches to natural wellness.

Research

Exploring questions at the intersection of traditional knowledge, natural wellness, and modern research.

Community

Creating opportunities for outreach, collaboration, shared learning, and participation.
