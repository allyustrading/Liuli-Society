Case studies

Learning from experience with context.

Case studies are educational records of context, observations, limitations, and questions—not proof of medical effectiveness.

FORMAT

Background

Describe the context and educational question being explored.

OBSERVATIONS

What was observed

Document observations carefully without converting individual experience into a general claim.

LIMITATIONS

What remains unknown

State uncertainties, alternative explanations, and boundaries of interpretation.

Important: Case studies do not establish diagnosis, treatment effectiveness, safety, or clinical efficacy.
